# Netra: enterprise subscription

Netra is available under an annual **enterprise subscription** for production use. One subscription covers product access, updates and upgrades, and the stated support level. There is no separate support surcharge.

| Plan | What you get | Coverage |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Enterprise Essentials | Initial production deployment: 50 nodes, 3 clusters, 1 site; updates and upgrades | 8x5; P1 critical response within 4 business hours |
| Enterprise | Regulated and multi-cluster teams: 200 nodes, 10 clusters, 3 sites; updates and upgrades | 24x7 P1 intake, one-hour response target |
| Enterprise Scale | Large estates: 1,000 nodes, 25 clusters, 10 sites; updates and upgrades | 24x7 P1, 30-minute response target, TAM |
| Sovereign / MSP | Air-gapped, sovereign and service-provider deployments (custom) | Mission-critical, contracted |

Plan names follow [Enterprise pricing](sales/enterprise-pricing.md). The first production transaction is a paid 90-day production pilot (up to 25 nodes and 2 clusters), credited to year one if the customer signs within 60 days.

Priced per edition, sized by managed nodes and clusters (the limit reached first applies). Enterprise editions are sized by managed nodes and clusters, and support is part of each edition.

## Terms

- Annual prepaid subscription is the default, with a 12-month minimum. Multi-year prepay is available.
- Minimums apply after any discounts.
- Implementation, migration, training and managed operations are separate services.
- At expiry, updates, downloads and support end.
- Response targets need a contracted scope and are not uptime guarantees.
- Existing signed agreements keep their agreed terms.
